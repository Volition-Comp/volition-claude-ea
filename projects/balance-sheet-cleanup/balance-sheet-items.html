Van-sale loss booked off the balance sheet

$12,255.90
Done BALANCED LEDGERS ADAN

Resolved in production. The account Gain/Loss on Disposal of Van (400104) was mistyped as Off-Balance-Sheet, so the April 2024 van-sale loss never reached the income statement. The CPA confirmed the loss belongs in 2024, so the account was re-typed to Other Expense. The loss now flows to the 2024 P&L (the entry is dated 2024-04-24), and on the current balance sheet it sits in Retained Earnings instead of the off-balance section.

  • Effect: 2024 net income is reduced by $12,255.90, recognizing the loss where it belongs; the off-balance section clears.
  • Source entry: MISC/2024/04/0005 — “Sale of Van and Loan Payoff,” 2024-04-24.

New report: Balance Sheet (Cash Basis, Adjusted)

Built & tested
Live in production ADAN

Deployed to production. A true cash-basis balance sheet, formatted like the QuickBooks statements Balanced Ledgers is used to. It strips the accrual accounts that shouldn’t appear on a cash-basis view, foots correctly, and unfolds to account-level detail. Find it under Accounting → Reporting → Balance Sheet, in the report selector.

  • Excludes: Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, Inventory Interim (both received and delivered), Bills to Receive, and Tax Payable Cash Basis Transition.
  • The cash-basis balancing figure is folded into Retained Earnings (QuickBooks style), so there is no separate plug line.
  • A report is a read-only view — deploying it changed no ledger data.
