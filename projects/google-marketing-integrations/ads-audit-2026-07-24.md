## TL;DR

## 1. Conversion tracking is a tangle (fix measurement first)
The account has ~35 conversion actions piled up over years, three generations deep:
- **Dead Shopify-era** purchase actions (REMOVED) still referencing `/collections/refrigeration`,
  `/collections/lifestyle` — old Shopify URLs.
- **Dead Universal Analytics goals still ENABLED**: "Transactions (All Web Site Data)", "Smart Goal",
  "LP Duration", "LP Pages/Session". UA stopped collecting data in July 2023 — these track nothing.
- **Multiple overlapping GA4 purchase actions**: "Volition Components - GA4 (web) purchase" AND
  "VC Purchases" (both GA4 custom purchase), plus "VC Add To Cart", "VC Begin Checkout", "VC Submit
  Lead", "Contact", "Calls from ads", etc.

Result: the "conversions" column mixes dead sources with live ones and likely double-counts or
under-counts. **We can't judge campaigns until there's one trusted purchase conversion.**
Cleanup: pick GA4 purchase as the single source of truth, set it primary, demote/remove the rest.

## 2. Keywords are attracting the wrong searches (start here)
The search-terms report shows we're paying for clicks that aren't our customer:
- **Refrigeration Packages** (an awareness campaign we're keeping) is matching: "refrigerant",
  "refrigerant supply", "weitron refrigerant" (people want the *chemical*), "carrier truck
  refrigeration", "reefer unit for box truck", "small truck refrigeration units" (trucks, not vans),
  "international", "thermo by product". Very little of this is "van refrigeration unit."
- **SEM- Products** is matching: "dual pane rv windows" (RV, not commercial van), competitor/brand
  terms "ranger design inc", "knapheide van shelving", "van windows direct", "smallvansbigworld".

Fixes (classic keyword audit, which Adan flagged):
- Add **negative keywords**: refrigerant, supply, carrier, box truck, international, rv, and
  competitor brand names where we don't want to pay.
- **Tighten match types** — several broad/phrase keywords are the leak; move the good ones to
  phrase/exact and prune.
- Biggest single spend: SEM- Products "Van Windows" (phrase+exact) = ~$64/mo, 31 clicks, 1 conv.
