The location's fate is set almost entirely by the ramp and margin — the two numbers Brian's proposal left open. Overhead barely moves it.

What's baked in, and where it comes from

  • Gross margin. Odoo accounting shows Broomfield gross margin rising 56.9% (2024) → 59.8% (2025). A conservative per-part calculation lands ~41%. The gap means some material cost isn't posting to COGS — worth reconciling — so the model defaults to a cautious 52%.
  • Revenue. Confirmed orders grew $1.45M (2024) → $1.80M (2025). Of 222 orders in 2025, 69 were sub-$1,000 warranty / online / service tickets (1.2% of revenue) that drag the raw average down; real upfit jobs averaged ~$14,200/order.
  • Staffing (loaded 1.18× for taxes/benefits). Techs $25/hr, apprentice $23/hr, ops-manager equivalent ~$74k — all current Odoo rates. Ramp: 1 tech from open, 2nd at Month 4, 3rd at Month 8; coordinator from Month 3; operating partner as GM throughout.
  • Insurance $16.8k/yr & utilities ~$15k/yr. Insurance reflects your new agent's 2026-YTD run-rate (~$16.6k annualized, down from $30k). Utilities sit between Broomfield's ~$8k and a Phoenix cooling premium.
  • Merchant/processing at 2.5% of revenue, marketing front-loaded ($5k launch, tapering to $1.2k/mo), plus rent, software, professional fees, and shop/office overhead.
Sources: Odoo production (sale.order, account.move.line, hr.employee, FY2024–25) and QuickBooks exports (2021–23). This is a planning model, not a guarantee — every input is an assumption until Phoenix has a signed lease, a real supplier-priced margin, and a quantified dealer pipeline.
