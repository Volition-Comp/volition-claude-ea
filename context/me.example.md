# About Me (template)

Copy this file to `context/me.md` and fill in your own details. Your `me.md` stays **local** to
your machine (it's gitignored), so Claude knows who *you* are without sharing it with the team or
acting as someone else.

- **Name:** <your name>
- **Role:** <your role at Volition>
- **Timezone:** <e.g. Mountain Standard Time (MST)>
- **What I do:** <your day-to-day focus>
- **My priorities:** <what you're driving right now>

Anything personal to how you like Claude to work for you can go here too.
