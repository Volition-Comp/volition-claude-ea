# How to Connect Odoo to Claude

A quick guide to linking your Odoo to the Claude app so Claude can pull up
customer, quote, and order info for you. Takes about 5 minutes. You only do this once.

**You'll need:** your Claude login, your Odoo login, and the connector link from Adan
(it looks like a web address). Ask Adan for the link before you start.

---

## Steps

1. **Open Claude** in your browser at claude.ai and sign in.

2. Click your **name or the gear icon** (bottom left) to open **Settings**.

3. Find **Connectors** in the settings menu, then click **Add custom connector**
   (it may say "Add" with a plus sign).

4. **Paste the link** Adan gave you into the box, and confirm.

5. Claude will pop up an **Odoo login screen**. Sign in with your normal Odoo
   email and password, the same one you use every day.

6. Odoo will ask if you want to **allow Claude to access your account**. Click
   **Allow** (or Approve / Authorize).

7. You're done. The connector should now show as **Connected** in your settings.

---

## Test that it worked

Start a new chat in Claude and type:

> What are my 5 most recent Odoo quotations?

If Claude shows you real quotes from Odoo, you're all set. If it says it can't
find Odoo or asks you to connect, go back and check the steps above, or grab Adan.

---

## Good to know

- **You only see what you're allowed to see.** Claude uses your own Odoo login,
  so it can only pull the same info your account already has access to.
- **It's read-only to start.** At first Claude can look things up but won't change
  anything in Odoo. Adan will turn on editing for specific people later.
- **Nothing to install.** No downloads, no setup on your computer. It all happens
  in the browser.
- **Stuck?** Message Adan. Don't guess or paste any passwords anywhere other than
  the official Odoo login screen in step 5.
